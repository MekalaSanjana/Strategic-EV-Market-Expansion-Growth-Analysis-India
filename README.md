# Strategic-EV-Market-Expansion-Growth-Analysis-India
This project presents a comprehensive Electric Vehicle (EV) Market Intelligence Dashboard built using Power BI.  The analysis evaluates India’s rapidly growing EV ecosystem and provides data-driven strategic recommendations for market expansion, competitive positioning, manufacturing feasibility, and long-term scalability.
The project simulates a real-world business case where AtliQ Motors (USA-based automotive company) aims to expand its EV presence in India.

🎯 Problem Statement

India is emerging as one of the fastest-growing EV markets due to:

1) Rising fuel prices
2) Environmental awareness
3) Government incentives & subsidies
4) Expanding charging infrastructure

However, despite holding 25% EV market share in North America, AtliQ Motors has less than 2% presence in India.

The objective of this dashboard is to:

Analyze EV sales trends & CAGR
Compare 2-Wheeler vs 4-Wheeler adoption
Evaluate state-wise penetration
Measure subsidy & infrastructure impact
Forecast EV sales until 2030
Recommend ideal manufacturing location

Provide actionable strategic insights

📊 Dashboard Structure

The dashboard is divided into three key sections:

1️⃣ Problem Understanding

Business challenge, market opportunity & analysis objectives.

2️⃣ Primary Analysis

- Total EV Sales & CAGR
- State-wise Penetration
- 2W vs 4W Market Split
- Top & Bottom Makers
- Quarterly Trends
- CAGR Analysis
- Seasonality Trends
- 2030 Sales Projection
- Revenue Growth Estimation

3️⃣ Strategic Intelligence

- Government Subsidy Impact
- Charging Infrastructure Correlation
- Manufacturing State Recommendation
- Brand Ambassador Strategy
- Top 3 Business Recommendations

📈 Key Insights

📊 EV CAGR: 93.9%

🛵 2-Wheelers dominate with 91%+ market share

📍 Maharashtra, Karnataka & Kerala lead in adoption

🔋 Strong correlation between charging stations & EV sales

📈 Projected EV Sales by 2030: 8.9M+ units

💰 Revenue growth (2023–24): 55%+

🏆 Strategic Recommendations
🏭 1. Manufacturing Location
Establish production in a high-subsidy, policy-stable state (Maharashtra identified as top candidate).

🛵 2. Focus on 2-Wheeler Segment
Higher adoption rate and faster revenue scalability.

🔌 3. Charging Infrastructure Partnerships
Collaborate with charging providers to reduce range anxiety and accelerate adoption.
